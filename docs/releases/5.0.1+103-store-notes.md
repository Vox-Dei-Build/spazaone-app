# Spaza One 5.0.1+103 store notes

Release target: Google Play Internal and Apple TestFlight.

Status: prepared for internal testing. Promotion to either production store
remains a separate decision after internal QA.

## Google Play — What's new

This update makes customer messaging more reliable. Conversation history now
recovers from individual malformed messages, pricing requests retry safely when
credentials expire, and clear recovery options keep transactions and payment
requests from being sent without a verified price.

## App Store — What's New

- Customer conversation history keeps valid messages visible when one provider
  record cannot be read.
- Messaging and pricing requests recover once from expired credentials and show
  a clear retry option if the service remains unavailable.
- Transaction confirmations can be saved without sending, while payment
  requests remain unsent until an authoritative price is available.
- Additional privacy-safe diagnostics make provider, connection and pricing
  failures easier to distinguish.

## Internal testing focus

- Open a customer with existing conversation history and confirm valid messages
  remain visible when a malformed synthetic record is returned.
- Force an expired Auth or App Check credential and confirm one refresh recovers
  without duplicating a message or debit.
- Simulate unavailable and invalid pricing for transaction confirmations. Verify
  Try again, Save without sending and Keep editing behave independently.
- Simulate unavailable pricing for a Pay Later payment request. Verify the
  request remains unsent and can be retried explicitly.
- Confirm successful recovery clears the conversation warning.
- Verify both internal builds report `5.0.1+103`, use package/bundle
  `com.tsepo.pasella`, and connect to Firebase project `pasella-ledger`.

## Release operator notes

- Distribution is limited to Google Play Internal and Apple TestFlight.
- CodeMagic workflows: `android-release` and `ios-release`.
- Release tag: `v5.0.1+103`; this one tag starts both workflows.
- `pubspec.yaml` must contain `5.0.1+103` at the tagged commit.
- The production backend contract must report the exact tagged app commit before
