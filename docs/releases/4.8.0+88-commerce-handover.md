# SpazaOne 4.8.0+88 commerce handover

Date: 13 August 2026

Authority: Vox Dei (`Vox-Dei-Build/spazaone-app` and
`Vox-Dei-Build/spazaone-bot`). Delta repositories are backup mirrors only.

## Implemented

- Permanent `spazaone-dev` lane with side-by-side SpazaOne Dev Android/iOS
  identities, environment fail-closed checks, Firebase services, provider Test
  modes, synthetic data, migration rehearsal and the separate SpazaOne
  Development Botpress ADK agent.
- Server-only Payments V2 intents, immutable provider-event inbox,
  reconciliation, settlements, refund cases, inventory reservations, supplier
  funding reservations, supplier fulfilments, operations alerts and audit
  operations. New financial amounts use integer cents and never credit
  `salesVirtualBalance`.
- South African settlement setup through Paystack bank validation and verified
  subaccount creation. Existing merchants opt in through Payment Setup; raw
  legacy bank details are never promoted into settlement authority. New
  merchants can use Cash/Pay Later immediately but cannot receive online
  settlements until setup is approved.
- Billable bank validation is protected by mandatory App Check, owner/admin
  authorization, explicit rejection of card-shaped request fields, exact
  deduplication, two-attempt daily/six-attempt lifetime merchant limits, a
  ten-attempt platform daily budget (maximum R30 at the published R3 tariff),
  security alerts and an automatic global verification circuit breaker.
- Provider validation also requires a short-lived, two-attempt
  preauthorization from a Spaza One admin. Successful provider evidence
  creates an inactive pending destination and can never auto-approve it.
- Billing separates Campaign Credits, Settlements, Payment Setup and any
  non-zero Legacy Balance. Campaign Credit purchases credit automatically from
  one signed webhook; support corrections use an audited admin adjustment.
- Owned-stock online payment with pre-payment atomic reservation, immutable
  before/after stock snapshots, signed-event application, direct settlement,
  refund ownership and required notification outbox.
- Full and partial customer account payment links with WhatsApp
  phone-to-customer binding and exactly-once ledger projection.
- Supplier quantity, live CJ quote/route/stock validation, profitability by
  payment channel, prefunded-balance reservation, Paystack split snapshot,
  idempotent CJ create/pay, provider-ambiguity recovery, automatic refund
  ownership, tracking and reconciliation.
- Canonical merchant/supplier order presentation and lifecycle copy.
- Sales → Online is now a truthful four-capability commerce hub for Campaign
  Credits, owned-stock orders, account payments and supplier orders. It shows
  buyer-safe readiness reasons, backend-approved channels, Payment Setup
  actions and combined owned/supplier order reporting without launch promises.
- Flutter and Botpress have independent per-purpose rollback controls beneath
  their existing presentation/master controls. Effective readiness is always
  the intersection of the client/deployment gate and authoritative backend
  readiness; neither client invents a payment channel.
- Botpress product search, popular/browse routes, 50/100-product pagination,
  images/descriptions, Back, quantity, multi-product cart, Add product,
  checkout wording, total price, typed/voice fallback and hosted Payments V2
  journeys. Once owned payments are ready, plain “EFT” routes to hosted Instant
  EFT instead of manual banking instructions.
