# Spaza One 4.8.5+101 store notes

Release target: Google Play Internal and Apple TestFlight.

Status: prepared for internal testing. Promotion to either production store
remains a separate release decision after internal QA.

## Google Play — What's new

This update refreshes Spaza One with clearer, more consistent screens for
customers, products, recorded sales, settings and wallet activity. Sign-in and
shop setup are simpler, banking details can be edited after setup, and the
WhatsApp catalogue status takes up less space. It also improves stability when
leaving sign-in and sales flows and when catalogue or pricing data is delayed.

## App Store — What's New

Spaza One now has a cleaner, more consistent workspace across customers,
products, recorded sales, settings and wallet activity. The sign-in and shop
setup experience is simpler and more professional, while banking details can
be edited after they are captured.

The WhatsApp catalogue status is quieter on the Products page and opens full
details only when needed. Recorded sales are easier to scan, stock invoice
images and PDFs open inside the app, and several lifecycle and delayed-data
failures have been fixed.

## Internal testing focus

- Start from a fresh install and confirm Login appears without an immediate
  notification-permission prompt or the retired Pasella rebrand notice.
- Complete first-time setup, dismiss and resume onboarding, then sign out and
  sign back in.
- Open Customers and customer Activity with empty, loading, populated and
  failure states.
- Review Products, stock filters and WhatsApp catalogue summary/details; check
  price and arrow alignment at standard and large text sizes.
- Create, edit and inspect recorded sales. Open image and PDF stock invoices
  and confirm every bottom sheet clears the home indicator and keyboard.
- Add banking details, return to Wallet, edit them and confirm the saved values
  reappear.
- Exercise Settings sections, short landscape, 320 px layouts and 2× text.
- Confirm both internal builds report `4.8.5+101` and use the production app
  identifiers and Firebase project `pasella-ledger`.
