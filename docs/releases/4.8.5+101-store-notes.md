# Spaza One 4.8.5+101 store notes

Release target: Google Play Internal and Apple TestFlight.

Status: prepared for internal testing. Promotion to either production store
remains a separate release decision after internal QA.

## Google Play — What's new

This update gives Spaza One a calmer, more consistent look while keeping the
familiar customer orders experience. Products, recorded sales, transactions,
wallet, settings and onboarding are easier to scan, banking details can now be
edited, loading feels smoother, and several crashes and unnecessary refreshes
have been fixed.

## App Store — What's New

Spaza One now has a warmer, more consistent workspace that keeps the familiar
customer experience. The customer orders layout has been restored,
while Products, recorded sales, transactions, wallet, settings and onboarding
are calmer and easier to scan.

Banking details can now be edited after setup, loading states are smoother and
more consistent, and repeated refreshes have been reduced. This release also
fixes several crashes around messaging, sign-in, sales and delayed data.

## Internal testing focus

- Start from a fresh install and confirm Login appears without an immediate
  notification-permission prompt or the retired Pasella rebrand notice.
- Complete first-time setup, dismiss and resume onboarding, then sign out and
  sign back in.
- Open Customers and confirm Pay Later, Orders, Activity and Summary match the
  approved production-aligned layouts in empty, loading, populated and failure
  states.
- Review the restored customer Orders layout and its filters, search, totals
  and order rows at standard and large text sizes.
- Review Products, stock filters and the quieter WhatsApp catalogue
  summary/details; check compact card height plus price and arrow alignment.
- Create, edit and inspect recorded sales, payments, Pay Later entries and
  transaction details. Confirm forms remain calm and readable with the
  keyboard open.
- Confirm recorded sales and other supported lists use consistent shimmer
  loading, then revisit them and check that unchanged data does not repeatedly
  refresh.
- Open image and PDF stock invoices and confirm every bottom sheet clears the
  home indicator and keyboard.
- Add banking details, return to Wallet, edit them and confirm the saved values
  reappear.
- Exercise Settings sections, short landscape, 320 px layouts and 2× text.
- Repeat sign-in, messaging, recorded-sale and catalogue/pricing delayed-data
  flows and confirm no new fatal errors are recorded.
- Confirm both internal builds report `4.8.5+101` and use the production app
  identifiers and Firebase project `pasella-ledger`.
