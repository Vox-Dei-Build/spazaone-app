# Spaza One 4.8.0+88 release communications

Release target: Google Play Internal and Apple TestFlight.

Status: prepared for internal testing. Do not send the merchant message or
promote either store build until the internal release gate passes.

## Google Play — What's new

Manage delivery and Pay Later from Order options, edit supplier products after
adding them to inventory, and review online and supplier orders more clearly.
This update also improves loading states, payment readiness guidance, payouts,
WhatsApp actions, and stability across small screens, rotation, keyboards, and
large text.

## App Store — What's New

Spaza One 4.8 adds clearer control over online ordering and supplier inventory.
Merchants can configure delivery and Pay Later, edit supplier-product markup
and storefront visibility, and pause or keep listings internal. Order details,
tracking, payouts, loading states, WhatsApp actions, and payment-readiness
guidance are easier to understand. The update also resolves layout and wallet
session crashes across small screens, landscape, large text, and keyboard use.
