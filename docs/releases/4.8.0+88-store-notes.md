# Spaza One 4.8.0+88 release communications

Release target: Google Play Internal and Apple TestFlight.

Status: prepared for internal testing. Do not send the merchant message or
promote either store build until the internal release gate passes.

## Google Play — What's new

Get paid and sell more with Spaza One 4.8. Accept secure online payments for
customer orders and payment requests once your payment setup is approved. Set
delivery fees and service areas, offer Pay Later, and manage these choices from
Order options. You can also edit supplier-product markup, control storefront
visibility, track orders and payouts more clearly, and enjoy improved loading
and stability.

## App Store — What's New

Spaza One 4.8 helps you get paid and run online sales from one place.

Once your payment setup is approved, you can accept secure online payments for
customer orders and send payment requests with a payment link. Configure
delivery, a flat delivery fee and service area, or offer Pay Later from Order
options. Your enabled choices also guide customers ordering through WhatsApp.

You can now edit supplier products after adding them to inventory: change the
markup, publish or remove them from your storefront, keep them internal, or
pause them without deleting the product.

This release also brings clearer next actions and tracking for online and
supplier orders, more useful payout and settlement information, improved
loading states, and better payment-readiness guidance. Stability is improved
across small screens, landscape, large text, keyboards, store switching, and
sign-in changes.
