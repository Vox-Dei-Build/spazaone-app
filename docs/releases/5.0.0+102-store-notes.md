# Spaza One 5.0.0+102 store notes

Release target: Google Play Internal and Apple TestFlight.

Status: prepared for internal testing. Promotion to either production store
remains a separate decision after internal QA.

## Google Play — What's new

Spaza One 5 brings a calmer, more consistent design across customers, products,
recorded sales, transactions, wallet, settings and sign-in. Customer Activity,
Pay Later and sales entries are easier to scan; setup is simpler; banking
details can be edited; loading is smoother; and fixes reduce crashes, failed
payment actions and unnecessary data refreshes.

## App Store — What's New

Spaza One 5 is a major usability and reliability update for everyday shop work.

- A warmer, consistent design makes customers, products, sales, wallet and
  settings faster to scan while retaining the familiar customer Orders layout.
- Customer Activity figures align clearly, and the Pay Later balance, payment
  request and repayment-plan actions use less space.
- Product cards, stock reports and WhatsApp catalogue status are more compact;
  the WhatsApp Store listing choice is easier to find when adding a product.
- Recorded sales entries align consistently, the Record sale form reveals
  detail progressively, and transaction and payment details are easier to read.
- Sign-in and first-time setup are simpler, and the retired Pasella rebrand
  notice no longer interrupts returning merchants.
- Banking details can now be edited after setup.
- Content-shaped shimmer loading replaces inconsistent wait states, repeated
  data refreshes are reduced, and crashes around sign-in, messaging, catalogue,
  sales and payment requests are contained.

## GitHub release summary

Spaza One 5.0.0 completes an app-wide approachable-design pass and preserves the
compact production layouts merchants preferred. It standardises typography,
spacing, navigation, forms, loading and error recovery across the active app;
refines Customer Activity, Pay Later and Recorded Sales alignment; restores the
production-led customer Orders experience; and makes banking details editable.

The release also hardens asynchronous sign-in, messaging, catalogue, sales and
payment-request flows. Firestore listeners and repeated queries are deferred,
coalesced or bounded where safe, with backend query/index support for the
affected scheduled, settlement and catalogue-status work.

## Internal testing focus

- Start from a fresh install and confirm the professional sign-in screen appears
  without the retired Pasella rebrand notice or an immediate notification prompt.
- Complete, leave and resume onboarding; sign out and back in; verify setup
  progress and existing merchant data remain intact.
- Review Customers, Orders, Activity, Summary and Pay Later in empty, loading,
  populated and failure states. Confirm Activity values share one right edge and
  Pay Later keeps its balance and two main actions compact.
- Add and edit products, check compact card alignment, stock reporting, the
  prominent WhatsApp Store listing choice and the concise catalogue status flow.
- Record and edit a sale, inspect aligned entries and details, and verify sheets
  clear the home indicator and keyboard.
- Add, edit and inspect payments and Pay Later transactions. Confirm pricing or
  messaging-provider failures show recoverable feedback instead of blocking or
  crashing the transaction.
- Add banking details, return to Wallet, edit them and confirm the saved values
  persist.
- Verify content-shaped shimmer loading across supported lists and revisit busy
  screens to confirm unchanged data is not repeatedly fetched.
- Repeat the critical flows on a small screen, in landscape and at 2× text size.
- Confirm both internal builds report `5.0.0+102`, use bundle/package
  `com.tsepo.pasella`, and connect to Firebase project `pasella-ledger`.
