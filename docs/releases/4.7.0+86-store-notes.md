# Spaza One 4.7.0+86 store notes

Release target: Google Play Internal and Apple TestFlight.

## Google Play — What's new

Billing is now cleaner and easier to scan, with compact balances, simpler
account actions and a redesigned Top Up screen. Dropshipping products are more
reliable to browse, save and add to your shop, with clearer order and delivery
steps. We also improved notification counts, product search, banking-detail
copying, app navigation, Android 16 support and overall stability.

## App Store — What's New

This update makes everyday shop management faster and clearer:

- Cleaner Billing balances, Account actions and Top Up choices.
- More reliable dropshipping catalogue browsing and saved products.
- Simpler dropshipping order, payment and delivery steps.
- Copyable banking details and clearer product search.
- Restored order and message notification counts.
- Improved navigation, app icon sizing and overall stability.

Pay online remains unavailable in this release while the payment feature is
being completed. WhatsApp top-ups continue to work as before.

## GitHub release summary

Spaza One 4.7.0 improves the merchant Billing experience, hardens the
dropshipping catalogue and fulfilment workflow, restores reliable unread
counters, and updates Android support to API level 36. Paystack remains
feature-flagged off. This tag publishes only to Google Play Internal and Apple
TestFlight for final device QA.
