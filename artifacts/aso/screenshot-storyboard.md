# SpazaOne ASO screenshot storyboard

## Production sets

- Apple iPhone 6.9-inch portrait: 1320 × 2868 px
- Google Play phone portrait: 1080 × 1920 px (9:16)
- Six screenshots per store, in the same narrative order
- English (UK) for Apple and English (US) for Google Play

## Visual system

- Apple: deep navy, white typography, warm multicolour ambient glow and a realistic dark phone frame
- Google Play: soft off-white, dark typography, subtle yellow/pink ambient colour and a realistic dark phone frame
- Poppins ExtraBold for the editorial headlines and Poppins ExtraBold Italic for the SpazaOne wordmark
- Yellow cart, navy, green and warm accent colours carried forward from the supplied Pasella artwork
- One benefit-led headline per frame
- No unsupported performance claims, ranking claims, prices or testimonials
- No real customer names, photos, numbers or balances

## Privacy gate

Only the six sources listed above may be uploaded to Figma. Raw Android captures that contained retained simulator data were deleted after review and must be recreated with dedicated demo data if they are needed later.

## Approval gate

Do not upload store assets, edit store metadata, remove the iOS version from review, submit for review, publish, deploy or merge without the user's explicit approval immediately before the action.
