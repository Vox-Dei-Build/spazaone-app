SpazaOne puts your whole shop in your pocket. It helps South African merchants run the daily work behind the counter from one phone.

Keep customer balances clear, take and manage WhatsApp orders, record sales, track products and stock, and send friendly payment reminders without relying on loose paper or memory.

WITH SPAZAONE YOU CAN

• Track what each customer owes
• Record payments and keep a clear history
• Take and manage WhatsApp orders
• See whether orders are paid, collected or still pending
• Add products and monitor stock
• Record sales, costs and profit
• Create reusable WhatsApp and SMS promotions
• Keep customers, orders and follow-up in one place

BUILT FOR EVERYDAY TRADE
